# Buchanan & Johnson (2011): comparative claustrum–insula morphology

Buchanan, K. J., & Johnson, J. I. (2011). Diversity of spatial relationships of the claustrum and insula in branches of the mammalian radiation. *Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences*, 1225(S1), E30–E63. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1749-6632.2011.06022.x

## Source and construction

- Local source: `Buchanan-2011-Diversity of spatial.pdf`, copied from the BSU Claustrum anatomy papers folder. SHA-256: `175ebe86ed3b946a1c59ed4a0697fd00636c5316b66cf470ec7adc92c490b6fa`.
- The paper has no 26-species numerical trait table. `Buchanan_Johnson_2011_ResultsText_snapshot.csv` is a **constructed snapshot**: AI-assisted visual transcription of specimen labels in Figures 2–8 (PDF pp. 5–30), then manual coding of explicit Results, Discussion and figure-caption statements on 2026-10-27. Each nonblank state has its evidence locator in the adjacent column.
- Figure labels supply the historical scientific names exactly as printed. There is one pictured specimen per taxon; observations are not estimates of within-species prevalence. The published digital images came from the Welker–Wisconsin, Yakovlev–Haleem and Johnson–Michigan State collections.
- The `.R` reader checks the fixed vocabulary and 26 unique names, strips evidence prose into the analysis CSV and writes a DOI-coded public TSV. The frozen snapshot is never regenerated by the reader.

## Coding rules

- Empty state = **not explicitly established**, never absence. `uncertain` is used for the manatee insula/claustrum interpretation, which the authors could not establish from available sections.
- `insula_operculation_state` is nominal (`none`, `proto`, `operculated`, `uncertain`), not a measured continuous or ordered score. `operculated` combines the authors' rudimentary, substantial and well-operculated examples; the evidence column retains the location.
- `opercular_cover_pattern` records only stated source of opercular cover. It is not a folding index.
- `anterior_claustral_bulb_observed` requires a rounded anterior or rostral head; a carnivore triangular top or dorsal enlargement alone does not qualify.
- `superior_triangular_claustral_expansion_observed` accepts the explicitly described triangular superior thickening at any depicted rostrocaudal level. A `no` is coded only for harbor seal, expressly contrasted by the authors.
- `superior_insula_claustrum_free` means any described superior insular region without underlying claustrum. It is distinct from total claustrum absence.

## Variation and overlap

The five coded traits vary among the specimens with explicit states. They are new anatomical characters relative to the repository's registered volume, neuron-density and whole-cortex gyrification variables. Kowiański's five morphology types are not assigned to these taxa; the two schemes are not automatically interchangeable. The dolphin islets described in Figure 8D overlap the Baizer bottlenose-dolphin observation and are retained only as a note, not a duplicate new species value. Manatee cell islets are putative and not coded as definite claustrum islands. The 25/26 claustral-root claim is not coded as a presence/absence comparison because the dolphin root could not be identified with the available morphology.
