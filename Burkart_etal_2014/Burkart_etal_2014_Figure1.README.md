## Interpretation and provenance

Fifteen species summaries represent 24 tested groups, including three human kindergarten groups (ages 4:5–7:1 years), not adult-human observations. The behavioral outcome is the percent of trials with transfers in the final two test sessions of phase IV by individuals passing control criterion 1. Species summaries overlap TableS7/roster observations. The allomaternal-care x-axis is a derived literature score described in Supplementary Table2, NOT a primary behavioral observation. Points were manually selected on a 400-dpi PDF-page-3 crop (325,250,549,410 points). Pixel calibration and selected coordinates are frozen in the snapshot; original crop is in reference_tables. Linear axes. Prosociality rounded to whole percentage points, allocare to 0.01; approximate reading uncertainty ±1 percentage point (not sampling error). Closely touching marker identities follow figure labels and x-axis ordering; original coordinates remain reviewable.
