## Interpretation and provenance

Twenty-one nonhuman groups, fourteen species; human group performance is not tabulated here. These are group totals, not individual trials. The percentages in columns 4/9 describe the share of transfers attributable to individuals meeting control criteria, NOT overall proactive prosociality. Overall species performance is captured separately from Figure1. Previous testing/colony reuse is not proof of an independent cohort relative to Burkart & van Schaik (2013); retain group identities before pooling. Main Table2 and TableS7 disagree in four noninfant group counts; neither is substituted for the other.
