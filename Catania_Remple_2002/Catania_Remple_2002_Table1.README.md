# Catania & Remple 2002 — Table 1

Source: Catania, K. C., & Remple, M. S. (2002). Somatosensory cortex dominated by the representation of teeth in the naked mole-rat brain. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 99*(8), 5692–5697. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.072097999

The printed Table 1 is frozen in `Catania_Remple_2002_Table1_snapshot.csv`; cells retain the paper's `percent of S1 (mm²)` convention. A second frozen text snapshot records the paper's approximate S1/neocortex comparison for naked mole-rats and the four Long–Evans rat hemispheres analyzed by the authors. The R script emits individual, mean, SD, and approximate comparison rows without converting one denominator into another.

The four naked mole-rat cases are adult animals. Rat S1 is reported only as approximately 21% of neocortex; no rat mm² value is invented. Public export remains pending registry-row paste.
