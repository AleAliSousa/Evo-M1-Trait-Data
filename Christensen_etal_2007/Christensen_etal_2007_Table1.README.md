# Christensen et al. 2007 — Table 1

“Neocortical and hippocampal neuron and glial cell numbers in the rhesus monkey,” *The Anatomical Record* 290:330–340, DOI 10.1002/ar.20504. The exact publisher PDF is in this folder. The source cohort is eight young rhesus monkeys (*Macaca mulatta*), four female and four male, designated controls in an interventional study; all received repeated general anesthesia. The paper gives mean age 2.8 ± 0.46 years and mean weight 3.7 ± 0.6 kg. The species label appears in article text rather than in the printed tables, and is linked through `_keys/General/species_key.csv`.

PDF page 4 (printed p. 333). The eight-row frozen CSV was manually transcribed from the rendered table on 2026-11-24, retaining printed region labels, sampling fractions, step lengths, counting-frame areas, section/disector counts and sums of sampled neuronal/glial profiles. The item reader separates x/y step dimensions and computes the section sampling fraction from printed `1/ssf`. `ΣQ` are **sampled profiles**, not estimated regional cell numbers. Cortical sampling was right-sided; the paper states that the hippocampal sample comprised four right and four left sides.

Run from repository root: `Rscript --vanilla Christensen_etal_2007/Christensen_etal_2007_Table1.R`. The reader uses only its frozen CSV, writes `Christensen_etal_2007_Table1.csv` and the DOI-coded public TSV by looking up `Item encoded` in `__ReadMeAddON.xlsx`. Definitions are in `reference_tables/Christensen_etal_2007_Table1_definitions.csv`. Eight method rows are retained for count provenance; do not merge the sampled-profile values as macaque neuron or glial totals. Human visual signoff of the manually transcribed snapshot remains pending. No figure-derived dataset was created.
