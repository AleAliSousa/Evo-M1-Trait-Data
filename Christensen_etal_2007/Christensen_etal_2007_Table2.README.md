# Christensen et al. 2007 — Table 2

“Neocortical and hippocampal neuron and glial cell numbers in the rhesus monkey,” *The Anatomical Record* 290:330–340, DOI 10.1002/ar.20504. The exact publisher PDF is in this folder. The source cohort is eight young rhesus monkeys (*Macaca mulatta*), four female and four male, designated controls in an interventional study; all received repeated general anesthesia. The paper gives mean age 2.8 ± 0.46 years and mean weight 3.7 ± 0.6 kg. The species label appears in article text rather than in the printed tables, and is linked through `_keys/General/species_key.csv`.

PDF page 8 (printed p. 337; rotated landscape table). The 21-row frozen CSV preserves seven neocortical regions, each with printed Average, St Dev, and CV rows and meaningful blanks. The reader produces one regional record per average and keeps the SD, CV, and CE columns separate. Counts printed in millions are converted to absolute cells by ×10⁶; density printed in 10⁶/mm³ is converted by ×10⁶; volume is in mm³ **after tissue processing**, without shrinkage correction. This right-neocortex result is from the same eight-monkey cohort as Table 4. Frontal subregions are nested within Frontal; Total neocortex contains Frontal, Temporal and parietal, and Occipital. They must not be added as separate animals or pooled as independent measures.

Run from repository root: `Rscript --vanilla Christensen_etal_2007/Christensen_etal_2007_Table2.R`. The reader uses only its frozen CSV, writes `Christensen_etal_2007_Table2.csv` and the DOI-coded public TSV by looking up `Item encoded` in `__ReadMeAddON.xlsx`. Definitions are in `reference_tables/Christensen_etal_2007_Table2_definitions.csv`. Checks: all printed average total-cell counts differ from neuron + glia by at most 1 million (Ventral prefrontal differs by 1, consistent with separately rounded columns). The three non-overlapping top regions sum exactly to the printed Total neocortex neuron and glial means. No volume-sum identity is imposed. The paper gives a shrinkage-corrected total volume of about 18,500 mm³ in text, distinct from the table’s processed 8,499 mm³. Use the source-specific processed status. Human visual signoff of the manually transcribed snapshot remains pending. No figure-derived dataset was created.
