# Christensen et al. 2007 — Table 3

“Neocortical and hippocampal neuron and glial cell numbers in the rhesus monkey,” *The Anatomical Record* 290:330–340, DOI 10.1002/ar.20504. The exact publisher PDF is in this folder. The source cohort is eight young rhesus monkeys (*Macaca mulatta*), four female and four male, designated controls in an interventional study; all received repeated general anesthesia. The paper gives mean age 2.8 ± 0.46 years and mean weight 3.7 ± 0.6 kg. The species label appears in article text rather than in the printed tables, and is linked through `_keys/General/species_key.csv`.

PDF page 9 (printed p. 338). The six-row frozen CSV preserves CA1 and CA2–3 Average, St Dev, and CV rows. The analysis gives two subregion records, with printed CE and density kept separate from SD and CV. Counts in millions and density in 10⁶/mm³ are converted to absolute cells and cells/mm³. Volume is processed tissue volume in mm³. The paper says four hippocampi were right-sided and four left-sided, so these group estimates have **mixed hemisphere** scope; they are not right-only or bilateral totals. The caption mentions a neuron/glia ratio, but the printed table has no such column and none was invented.

Run from repository root: `Rscript --vanilla Christensen_etal_2007/Christensen_etal_2007_Table3.R`. The reader uses only its frozen CSV, writes `Christensen_etal_2007_Table3.csv` and the DOI-coded public TSV by looking up `Item encoded` in `__ReadMeAddON.xlsx`. Definitions are in `reference_tables/Christensen_etal_2007_Table3_definitions.csv`. CA1 and CA2–3 are subregions and do not constitute a printed whole-hippocampus total. Do not add them to Table 2 neocortex or relabel them as a unilateral right hippocampus. Human visual signoff of the manually transcribed snapshot remains pending. No figure-derived dataset was created.
