# Christensen et al. 2007 — Table 4

“Neocortical and hippocampal neuron and glial cell numbers in the rhesus monkey,” *The Anatomical Record* 290:330–340, DOI 10.1002/ar.20504. The exact publisher PDF is in this folder. The source cohort is eight young rhesus monkeys (*Macaca mulatta*), four female and four male, designated controls in an interventional study; all received repeated general anesthesia. The paper gives mean age 2.8 ± 0.46 years and mean weight 3.7 ± 0.6 kg. The species label appears in article text rather than in the printed tables, and is linked through `_keys/General/species_key.csv`.

PDF page 9 (printed p. 338). The ten-row frozen CSV preserves four female records, a female Average, four male records, and a male Average, including the dash for the first female processed volume. The analysis gives the eight animals a within-sex source sequence (no specimen IDs are printed) and labels the two averages as summaries rather than extra animals. Counts in millions are converted to absolute cells; one source volume remains missing. The printed sex-average neuron/glia ratios are means of individual ratios rather than ratios of displayed group-mean counts.

Run from repository root: `Rscript --vanilla Christensen_etal_2007/Christensen_etal_2007_Table4.R`. The reader uses only its frozen CSV, writes `Christensen_etal_2007_Table4.csv` and the DOI-coded public TSV by looking up `Item encoded` in `__ReadMeAddON.xlsx`. Definitions are in `reference_tables/Christensen_etal_2007_Table4_definitions.csv`. Checks: individual displayed neuron/glia ratios agree with the displayed component counts to 0.01; displayed total counts differ from neuron + glia by no more than 1 million (the male Average is 2,230 vs 1,425 + 806 = 2,231, a rounding difference). This is the same right-neocortex cohort as Table 2 Total neocortex. Do not pool the two items as independent macaque observations.
