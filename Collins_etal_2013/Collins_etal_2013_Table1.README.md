# Collins et al. 2013, Table 1

Collins, C. E., Leitch, D. B., Wong, P., Kaas, J. H., & Herculano-Houzel, S. (2013). Faster scaling of visual neurons in cortical areas relative to subcortical structures in non-human primate brains. *Brain Structure and Function*, 218, 805–816. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00429-012-0430-5).

## Source and scope

The exact EndNote attachment is copied here as `Collins-2013-Faster-scaling-of-visual-neurons.pdf`. Table 1 is on printed page 807/PDF page 3. The snapshot retains all ten printed species rows and the final Papio/Callithrix variation row, including source-order columns, `n.a.`, mean ± SD, and ×10^6/×10^9 count units. Although the paper discusses eleven species, ten species are printed in Table 1. The final variation row is a derived ratio and is retained only in the snapshot.

The reader creates 81 available structure-by-measure rows. SC, LGN, V1 and MT rows are marked primary; whole-brain and cortical gray-matter reference values are marked secondary because the table credits Herculano-Houzel et al. 2007/2008, Gabi et al. 2010, and selected masses from Stephan et al. 1981. All measurements refer to a single hemisphere. Masses are in g; printed neuron counts and SDs are converted from ×10^6 or ×10^9 to absolute counts. Missing values remain absent. Specimen overlap with related Collins and Herculano-Houzel datasets requires review before merging.

For Charvet et al. Figure 7, the macaque V1 row contributes 1.880 ± 0.085 g and 213.76 ± 54.27 million neurons (SD). The mass-to-volume step is recorded in `../Charvet_etal_2013/internal_checks/Charvet_etal_2013_Figure7_Collins2013_point.README.md`; the source table does not directly measure volume.

Run from repository root: `Rscript --vanilla Collins_etal_2013/Collins_etal_2013_Table1.R`. The reader writes the local CSV and DOI-coded public TSV.
