# Elston & Fujita 2014 — documented skip (review; only reproduced, plotted data)

Elston, G. N., & Fujita, I. (2014). Pyramidal cell development: postnatal spinogenesis, dendritic
growth, axon growth, and electrophysiology. *Frontiers in Neuroanatomy*, 8, 78.
doi:10.3389/fnana.2014.00078. PDF in this folder: `Elston-2014-Pyramidal cell devel.pdf` (20 pp.).

## Screening result (28 Oct 2026, AI assistant)
- **No tables.** The text layer (born-digital PDF) contains no `Table` element; all 15 items are figures.
- **No new data.** Every quantitative figure is credited to earlier work in its legend
  ("Modified from", "Data taken from", "Based on data from", "reproduced from"): Figs 1, 2, 5–7,
  9–14. Figs 3, 4, 8 and 15 are schematics/photomicrographs.
- **No printed values.** The plotted values (synapse densities, dendritic-tree size, branching,
  peak spine density, spine totals per cell across 8 species in Fig. 13, V1-size scaling in Fig. 14)
  are bars/points without numbers; a figure digitisation here would re-ingest compiled secondary
  values, which the house rules forbid.

So no item is created and no registry row is proposed.
