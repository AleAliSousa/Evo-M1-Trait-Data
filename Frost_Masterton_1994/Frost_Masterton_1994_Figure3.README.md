# Frost & Masterton 1994 — Figure 3

Frost and Masterton (1994), *Hearing Research* 76:67–72, doi:10.1016/0378-5955(94)90088-4. Local source `Frost_Masterton_1994.pdf`, Figure 3 on printed p.70/PDF p.4. The figure compares four averaged audiograms. Only the two **new measurements from this paper** were extracted: `d` = *Monodelphis domestica* and `e` = *Marmosa elegans*. The `v` *Didelphis virginiana* and `r` laboratory rat curves are explicitly credited to Ravizza, Heffner & Masterton 1969 and Kelly & Masterton 1977, and are omitted to avoid secondary duplicate measurements. Source series identity and the frequency order are retained.

The existing `ResultsText` item reports hearing ranges and best thresholds, not the full per-frequency curves. Figure 3 therefore adds distinct primary observations. `Frost_Masterton_1994_Figure3_snapshot.csv` records marker-center positions read from a 250-dpi source-page render, the 0/90 dB axis anchors, source order, and the two primary series. `Rscript --vanilla Frost_Masterton_1994/Frost_Masterton_1994_Figure3.R` writes the 18-row CSV and DOI-coded public TSV. Values are approximate graph readings in dB SPL, not the authors' underlying raw thresholds. The crowded 64–85 kHz points have greater visual uncertainty; the 85 kHz readings agree with the Results text (86 dB for *Monodelphis*, 78 dB for *Marmosa*) within about 0.5 dB. No error bars are printed in Figure 3. The x axis is logarithmic; the caption's frequency unit is Hz, represented here as kHz with an explicit column label.

Run `Rscript --vanilla Frost_Masterton_1994/Frost_Masterton_1994_Figure3_replot.R` to read the extracted CSV and save `Frost_Masterton_1994_Figure3_replot.png`. The replot preserves the two primary series, source frequency order, logarithmic frequency axis, dB SPL axis, and the 60 dB guide.
