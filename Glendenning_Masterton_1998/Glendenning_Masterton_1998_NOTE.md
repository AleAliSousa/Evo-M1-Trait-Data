## Why Table 8 is not an item

Table 8 counts *genera per order* in the world fauna ("cf. Nowak, 1991", p. 83) and in this paper's 53-genus sample, with the percentage deviations. It describes the sampling design, not a trait of any species: there is no species-level value to join to the comparative database. Its only external numbers (genera per order) are reproduced from Nowak (1991). No snapshot, CSV or TSV was made for it.

## Secondary sources

The only per-value citations in the data tables are in Table 1 (body weights from Nowak [1991]; bat body/brain weights from D. Wong) — see `reference_tables/Glendenning_Masterton_1998_Table1_references.csv`. Tables 2–7 are computed from Table 1 and cite no sources, so they have no references file.

## Errata

`reference_tables/Glendenning_Masterton_1998_errata.csv` — 8 proposed rows: Table 1 rows whose nuclei do not sum to the printed Total (Chinchilla — duplicated Guinea-pig row; Mouse, spiny; Opossum, didelphis; Mouse, small) and the same four rows in Table 2, which do not sum to 100%.
