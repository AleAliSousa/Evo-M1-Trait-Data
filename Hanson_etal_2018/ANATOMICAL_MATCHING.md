# Anatomical matching — 12 November 2026

Source: `Hanson_etal_2018.pdf`, anatomical delineation and stereological counting Methods (caudate, associative putamen, nucleus accumbens).
The caudate measurements sample dorsal and medial territories; putamen is the rostral associative territory, not the entire putamen. Accumbens boundaries are based on cytoarchitecture and morphology; the paper discusses imperfectly sharp boundaries. These are cell densities in samples within named structures, not whole-structure total counts.

The analysis averages dorsal/medial caudate densities with equal weights. This is a sample summary, not a volume-weighted caudate estimate. Heiss 2004 uses small manually placed spheres and does not establish which of these subdivisions they cover. Retain only as sampled-structure comparisons and label this uncertainty; never claim identical masks or a whole-nucleus density census.
