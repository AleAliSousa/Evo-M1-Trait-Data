# Harvey & Clutton-Brock 1985 — Table 1

Harvey, P. H., & Clutton-Brock, T. H. (1985). Life history variation in primates. *Evolution* 39(3):559–581. DOI 10.1111/j.1558-5646.1985.tb00395.x. The exact publisher PDF `Harvey-1985-Life History Variation in Primates.pdf` is held here. Table 1 spans PDF pages 4–8 (printed pages 562–566) and gives 14 numbered measurements for 135 primate species.

`Harvey_CluttonBrock_1985_Table1_snapshot.csv` is a frozen transcription of the complete table. It preserves all 135 species in source order, the 23 family/subfamily heading rows, repeated numbered headers and page continuation markers, original dashes, and original units. The PDF has a selectable text layer but the table is rotated. Glyph coordinates were used to align rows and columns; 29 text-layer errors were corrected against the rendered pages, with the before/after record in `reference_tables/Harvey_CluttonBrock_1985_Table1_transcription_notes.csv`. This includes broken species spacing, `III` for printed `111`, `1.l0` for `1.10`, and dashes omitted from two rows. The source snapshot is frozen; do not rerun extraction or overwrite it as part of an analysis build. Human visual signoff of the full table remains pending.

Run from repository root: `Rscript --vanilla Harvey_CluttonBrock_1985/Harvey_CluttonBrock_1985_Table1.R`. The R reader reads only its frozen CSV and writes `Harvey_CluttonBrock_1985_Table1.csv` and the DOI-coded public TSV, using `Item encoded` in `__ReadMeAddON.xlsx`. The analysis retains 135 source taxon labels and the printed family/subfamily context. Female and male body weight are converted from kg to g; neonatal and adult brain weight from g to mg. The other ten measures retain the source units. Dashes become missing values. The table yields 129 adult brain masses. Variable definitions are in `reference_tables/Harvey_CluttonBrock_1985_Table1_definitions.csv`.

These species averages are **secondary literature data**. Table 1 gives no per-row citations to the underlying measurements, and historical taxon names have not been reconciled. `Species` is therefore identical to `species_as_published`; no species identity or taxonomic order was inferred. Do not pool these rows as independent primary measurements. Use the item for source comparison and provenance tracing, and verify source concepts before any species-level merge. No figure-derived dataset was created, so no replot applies.
