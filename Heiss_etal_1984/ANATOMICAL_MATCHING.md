Methods p. 213: seven awake healthy male volunteers, CT-guided outlined PET ROIs, approximately 8-mm FWHM with 11-mm sections; larger units combine ROIs across slices. Tables 1 and 3 summarize 43 side-specific sites as 22 bilateral/midline regions. Thalamus is a broad sampled region, not an identified medial nucleus or a complete modern MRI mask. Table 3 gives sampled thalamic volume about 4.0 cm3; do not equate it to entire anatomical thalamus. Midbrain and lentiform nucleus are composite territories, not individual nuclei. Cerebellar cortex, dentate and vermis are separate; cingulate and lobar white matter are available.

Heiss 2004 reference 21 cites this paper. Its older comparison column accounts for differing lumped constants; do not call it 1991 or silently replace native Table 3 kinetic/autoradiographic values.
