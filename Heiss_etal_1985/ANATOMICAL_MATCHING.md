# Anatomical and physiological scope — 12 November 2026

Source: `heiss_etal_1985.pdf`, pp. 362–363, Methods and Table I.
The study evaluates four men (three nondreamers and one dreamer), with repeated waking and sleep scans after about 28 hours on duty with little/no sleep. Two of six initially studied subjects were excluded. Awake data are a sleep-deprived waking condition, not a rested normative cohort.

Seven-slice PET, about 8-mm in-plane FWHM and 11-mm slices; regional procedure refers to Heiss 1984. Broad thalamus and lentiform nucleus are not medial thalamus and putamen respectively. Nuclear composition and full modern segmentation boundaries are not established. Use waking and sleeping rates separately, with the dreamer kept identifiable. This is a small state-sensitivity dataset, not a replacement for the higher-resolution 2004 primary rates. Participant overlap with 1984 is unknown.
