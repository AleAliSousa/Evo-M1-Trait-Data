# Thalamic anatomy — Heiss et al. 2004

The paper does **not** report a measured whole-thalamus mean in Table 1. It reports three separately sampled territories: `Nucleus medial thalami` (36.6 ± 4.72), `Corpus geniculatum laterale` (23.1 ± 3.33), and `Corpus geniculatum mediale` (20.2 ± 2.87), in µmol/100 g/min. `Nucleus subthalamicus` is a separate structure and must not be included in a thalamus aggregate.

## Primary evidence

- `Heiss_etal_2004.pdf`, printed p. 1812 / PDF p. 2, Methods: small noncortical structures were sampled with manually positioned spherical MRI VOIs referenced to anatomical atlases. Radii ranged across structures from 1 to 4 mm; larger structures could have up to three VOIs. The paper does not give a thalamus-specific radius or complete mask.
- Printed p. 1813 / PDF p. 3, Table 1: the exact labels and values above; both-hemisphere mean and interhemispheric differences are reported.
- The abstract uses the shorter word thalamus for 36.6, but Table 1 identifies that value as `Nucleus medial thalami`. Preserve the more precise table label.

## Interpretation and limits

The medial-thalamic label suggests medial nuclear sampling, but the paper does not explicitly equate its VOI with the entire histologically delineated mediodorsal nucleus. Do not silently relabel it as whole thalamus, or declare an exact match to Karlsen's MDT or Siletti's MD+Re.

The analysis project's arithmetic mean (36.6 + 23.1 + 20.2)/3 = 26.6333 (rounded to 26.6) is a **derived three-territory proxy**, not a published measurement or a volume-weighted whole-thalamus rate. It omits other territories and gives the two geniculate bodies two-thirds of the weight. The older value 29.9 (Heiss et al. 1984, reference 21) printed in the same row does not establish that the older ROI boundaries were identical.

## Consequence for matching

Keep the three source rows separate. Whole-thalamus MRI volumes or whole-atlas SV2A ROIs are not exact anatomical matches. Nuclear histology may offer a closer comparison, but the medial VOI boundaries remain unverified. A whole-thalamus metabolic analysis needs an appropriately defined rate source, or an explicitly labelled sensitivity proxy.

## Attribution correction — 12 November 2026

Reference 21 is Heiss et al. (1984), JCBFM 4:212–223, not 1991. The 2004 discussion (p. 1814) says these comparison values account for differing lumped constants. The primary 1984 paper labels a broad thalamus ROI and mixed midbrain; its entries cannot be relabelled as medial thalamus, substantia nigra or red nucleus based only on their placement in the 2004 table.
