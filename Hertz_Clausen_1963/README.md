## Candidate item: Table 1

Printed page 530 (PDF page 5) gives 14 calf central-nervous-system tissue rows,
including frontal, occipital, motor, sensory and cerebellar cortex, cerebral
white matter, caudate, mesencephalon, pons, medulla and spinal cord. Each row
reports oxygen uptake without and with added KCl and the stimulation percent.
The printed unit is **µmoles O₂/g fresh weight/hour**. Parentheses give tissue
sample counts, not animal counts or an error-bar type; the paper studied 21
calves in total and sometimes used multiple samples from one animal. The
with-KCl arm is an ex-vivo stimulation condition, not baseline physiology.
