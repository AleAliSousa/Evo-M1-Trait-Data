## Who read the values, when, and how it was checked

**Hand transcription** (HOWTO method 5) by an AI assistant (Claude) on 2026-11-18 from 170–200 dpi renders of PDF pages 1, 2, 3, 5 and 9 (journal pp. 451, 452, 453, 455, 459; `pdfplumber`), typed as a verbatim printed-text block into `Hevner_WongRiley_1992_Text_snapshot.R` (freezes, refuses to overwrite a differing copy).
