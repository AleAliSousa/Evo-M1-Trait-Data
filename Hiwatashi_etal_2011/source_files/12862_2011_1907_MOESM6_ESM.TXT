Additional file 6 for "Gene conversion and purifying selection shape nucleotide variation in gibbon L/M opsin genes" by T. Hiwatashi et al.

A "sequential" (fasta) format of the sequence alignments for the gibbon L and M opsin gene exon 5.

Species name abbreviations:
Hag, Hylobates agilis; Hla, H. lar; Hpi, H. pileatus; Nle, Nomascus leucogenys; Ssy, Symphalangus syndactylus.

Sequence names can be interpreted as in the following examples.
In 1_Hag_M_L, 1 stands for individual ID number, Hag for Hylobates agilis, M for male, L for L opsin gene.
In 2_Hag_F_L_1, F stands for female, L_1 for a tentative sequence type 1 of the L opsin gene.
In 57_Hag_M_M_1, M_1 stands for a tentative sequence type 1 of the M opsin gene.
