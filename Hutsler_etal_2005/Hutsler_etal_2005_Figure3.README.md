# Hutsler et al. 2005 — Figure 3

Species-level primary somatosensory cortical and laminar thicknesses digitized from the embedded
646 × 834 JPEG. The snapshot stores reviewed pixel boundaries and axis calibration; the `.R` script
converts them to µm and 0–1 proportions.

Absolute values are rounded to 10 µm with a conservative ±25 µm boundary uncertainty. Proportions
are rounded to 0.001 with ±0.010 uncertainty. Capybara's layer-IV segment is indistinguishable from
the plot boundary; the final table does not expose layer IV separately.

The resulting order means do not reproduce the narrative or Figure 6 summaries. These rows are
therefore provisional figure-derived data and must remain separate from exact reported summaries.
