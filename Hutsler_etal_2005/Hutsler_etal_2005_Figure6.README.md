# Hutsler et al. 2005 — Figure 6

Nine order × region bars digitized from the embedded 591 × 788 JPEG: primate, carnivore and rodent ×
motor, premotor and sensory cortex. Values are order means across the 13-species regional subset;
mouse is excluded.

Panel A's axis is absolute thickness (µm), panel B's axis is proportional thickness, and both panels'
bar heights reconcile as supragranular II/III. The printed caption is internally inconsistent, so the
interpretation and warning are explicit columns. Approximate uncertainties are ±10 µm and ±0.005.

These are the paper's closest M1-compatible data, but they are **not species-level M1 values**.
