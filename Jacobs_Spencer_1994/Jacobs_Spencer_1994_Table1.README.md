# Jacobs_Spencer_1994_Table1

Jacobs, L. F., & Spencer, W. D. (1994). Natural space-use patterns and hippocampal size in kangaroo rats. Brain, Behavior and Evolution, 44, 125–132. https://doi.org/10.1159/000113584

Table 1 (p.127): seven overlapping summaries of 22 animals, not seven independent species means. Merriam’s and bannertail rats each have five males and four females; Ord’s has four males. All animals were adult, collected near Portal, Arizona in March 1989.

Methods pp.126–127: saline/10% buffered formalin perfusion, trimmed caudal to cerebellum and weighed after fixation; frozen horizontal 80-μm sections, every fourth Nissl stained. Hippocampal boundaries include dentate gyrus and Ammon’s horn. Serial whole-brain sections were integrated with a truncated-cone formula to yield total hippocampal volume. No correction or extra bilateral doubling is added. Brain g becomes mg; body remains g and hippocampal volume mm³. SE is preserved as SE. Relative-size columns are derived metadata, distinct from the paper’s separate allometric expected/observed comparisons. Weighted group means are checked at reported precision.
