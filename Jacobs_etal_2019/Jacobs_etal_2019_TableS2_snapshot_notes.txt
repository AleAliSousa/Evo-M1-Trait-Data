Table S2 List of plant species and plant parts foraged and fed on by E. rubriventer between September 2012 and May 2013 at three sites in RNP. Table includes the percentage of foraging bouts accounted for by each plant taxon overall (% All) and for each site (TK = Talatakely, VL = Valohoaka, VT = Vatoharanana). Plant parts/species included in color modeling analyses are highlighted in grey. Ripeness of fruit is identified in bold if it was consumed in >50% of fruit foraging bouts when both unripe and ripe were consumed. Plant parts identified with “?” were present during a bout, but had not been directly observed to be consumed. (FR = Fruit, RFR = Ripe Fruit, MR = Mid-Ripe Fruit, UFR = Unripe Fruit, BD = Flower Bud, FL = Flower, YL = Young Leaf, ML = Mature Leaf)
