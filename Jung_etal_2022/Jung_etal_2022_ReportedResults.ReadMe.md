# Jung et al. (2022), reported quantitative results

## Source
Jung, Y. J., et al. (2022). Orientation pinwheels in primary visual cortex of a highly visual marsupial. *Science Advances, 8*, eabn0954. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abn0954

## Scope
This item contains quantitative wallaby results explicitly stated in the main article text and figure captions. It is not a reconstruction of supplementary Tables S1 or S2 because those tables were not included in the supplied files.

## Provenance
Primary wallaby measurements and secondary comparative/retinal values are distinguished in `data_role`. Associated statistics and individual values are retained in `note`. Full references directly supporting secondary values and methods are supplied in `reference_tables/Jung_etal_2022_secondary_references.csv`.

## Important attachment note
The two supplied files named `1-s2.0-S0960982220317383-mmc1.pdf` and `...-mmc3.pdf` belong to Ho et al. (2021), DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2020.11.027, not to Jung et al. (2022). They are retained in `related_sources/` as a cited comparative source, not treated as the Jung supplementary material.
