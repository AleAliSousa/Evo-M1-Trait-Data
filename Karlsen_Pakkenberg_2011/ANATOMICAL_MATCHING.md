# Anatomical matching — 12 November 2026

Source: `Karlsen_Pakkenberg_2011.pdf`, Methods defining neocortex and lobe sampling; accompanying author data.
Neocortex and four lobes refer to cortical grey matter. Limbic exclusions include cingulate gyrus, uncus, subiculum and entorhinal cortex. These histological definitions are not necessarily identical to Heiss's automatically segmented cortical templates.

The analysis uses bilateral counts divided by bilateral uncorrected volume from author data (ratio of group means). Keep this preparation basis separate from the shrinkage-corrected Table 2 volumes. The parent neocortex value overlaps its four lobes: preserve it descriptively but do not include it as an independent regional point alongside them. Lobe comparisons are named-structure/tissue matches, not voxel-equivalent samples.
