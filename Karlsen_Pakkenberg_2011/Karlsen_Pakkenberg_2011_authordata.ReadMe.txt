Karlsen & Pakkenberg 2011 - author-provided neocortical stereology workbooks
DOI: 10.1093/cercor/bhr033

Source
  Full Control and Down syndrome counting workbooks provided by B. Pakkenberg.
  Public redistribution is release-gated on written permission covering the workbook contents.
  Private correspondence is not included in this repository.

Snapshots
  Karlsen_Pakkenberg_2011_authordata_control_snapshot.xlsx
  Karlsen_Pakkenberg_2011_authordata_DS_snapshot.xlsx
  Complete macro-free copies of the supplied workbooks. All scientific sheets, formulas/results,
  labels and formatting are retained; VBA, external links, document properties and personal filenames
  were removed. Counts are one hemisphere; A = Control and B = Down syndrome.

Data readable (USE THIS)
  Karlsen_Pakkenberg_2011_authordata.R -> Karlsen_Pakkenberg_2011_authordata.csv
  Reads both Summ sheets, multiplies cell numbers by 2 for bilateral totals, leaves densities/ratios
  unchanged, and writes group means for five neocortical regions (Control n=6; DS n=4).
