# Thalamic anatomy — Karlsen et al. 2014

This is a **mediodorsal thalamic nucleus (MDT)** stereological study, not a whole-thalamus study.

## Primary evidence

- `Karlsen-2014-A stereological study of the medi.pdf`, printed p. 254 / PDF p. 2, Delineation of the MDT nucleus: cyto- and myeloarchitectonic criteria follow Hirai and Jones (1989). The authors describe rostral, lateral, caudal and medial boundaries, use adjacent myelin-stained sections as guidance, and check/redraw demarcations before counting.
- Printed p. 255 / PDF p. 3, Fig. 1: rostral, middle and caudal levels with MDT outlines in control and DS brains.
- Table 2 reports unilateral MDT reference volume and neuron/glial estimates. The reference volume is not shrinkage-corrected (Methods, printed p. 256); do not substitute a whole-thalamus or shrinkage-corrected denominator.

## Consequence for matching

Retain MDT as a nucleus-specific anatomical concept. A comparison with Heiss 2004's `Nucleus medial thalami` is plausible at the medial-thalamic level but **not yet demonstrated to be an exact delineation match**: Heiss does not supply a histological MDT boundary. Siletti MD is also potentially relevant; MD+Re additionally includes reuniens and must not be treated as the same tissue without qualification. Whole-thalamus MRI volumes, FreeSurfer SV2A means and Sjölin's unspecified tissue sample are distinct observations.
