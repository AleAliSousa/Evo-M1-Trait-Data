# Krubitzer et al. 1997 — Results and Discussion text

Source: Krubitzer, L., Künzle, H., & Kaas, J. H. (1997). Organization of sensory cortex in a Madagascan insectivore, the tenrec (*Echinops telfairi*). *Journal of Comparative Neurology, 379*(3), 399–414. https://doi.org/10.1002/(SICI)1096-9861(19970317)379:3%3C399::AID-CNE6%3E3.0.CO;2-Z

`Krubitzer_etal_1997_ResultsText_snapshot.csv` preserves eight approximate measurements or bounds stated in the Results and Discussion: total neocortical surface (one hemisphere), PV/S2 area, the head-related fraction of S1, S1 and auditory-field linear dimensions, and the paper's lower-bound count of processing divisions. Approximate values and inequalities remain explicit; linear dimensions are not converted to areas. The field called V is not assigned a V1 surface area because the paper treats the homology as tentative and does not print its area.
