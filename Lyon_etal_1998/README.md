# Lyon et al. 1998 — source only

The PDF was copied from `Flatmap_SurfaceArea_Project/papers_to_do/Cortical Image References/lyon_etal_1998.pdf`.

The primary paper “Cortical Connections of Striate and Extrastriate Visual Areas in Tree Shrews” (*Journal of Comparative Neurology* 401:109–128) reports tracer-label spread and patch spacing. These describe connectivity around injections rather than cortical-field surface area, so they are excluded from the area dataset.
