# Mangold-Wirz (1966), Table II

The `_source.tsv` transcribes the printed regional cerebralisation indices and percentages on pp. 462–464 of the PDF. The local CSV and public TSV are generated by `MangoldWirz__1966_TableII.R` (since 2026-10-31; it replaces the Table II part of `build_tables.py`, reproduces both files byte for byte, and takes the TSV name from the registry's `Item encoded`). `NI`, `RhI`, `CI`, and `StI` are dimensionless indices; the four `_pct` columns are percentages. These derived measures are retained as historical comparative data. They are not regional volume measurements and are not eligible for the mass or volume merges.

Species names and groups are printed labels, without automatic modern taxonomic reassignment. Table I of the same paper supplies the total-brain weights and index; no weights are duplicated into this table.

The paper's preface (pp. 449–450) describes these species-level data as an expansion of Wirz's 1950/1954 work, using additional material assembled at the Zoological Institute in Basel. This establishes a shared research lineage, but Table II does not provide a specimen or primary-source key for every value. The asterisk note under Table I identifies some entries taken from Stephan; it should not be projected onto Table II without row-level evidence.

The separate regional-index and proportion comparison, including the Dunbar-related provenance audit, is in [`comparison/`](comparison/README.md).
