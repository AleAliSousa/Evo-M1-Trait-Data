## Candidate item

The paper analyzes 123 endocasts from living apes and fossil hominins. Methods
describe measured endocranial volume (ECV) and 16 landmarks plus bilateral
semilandmarks per specimen. The Supplementary Material describes `DATA.rda`
with specimen-level ECV values and landmark arrays, and an annotated `script.R`
(Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.3931032`). The ECV vector is a real candidate item;
shape coordinates require a separate item decision. Supplementary Tables S1
(specimen list) and S2 (landmark definitions) are supporting metadata.
