# Meyer__1981_TableII

Meyer, J. (1981). A quantitative comparison of the parts of the brains of two Australian marsupials and some eutherian mammals. Brain, Behavior and Evolution, 18, 60–71. https://doi.org/10.1159/000121776

Table II spans pp.62–63: all 13 columns are transcribed in their printed order for seven taxa (91 region–taxon means). The joined snapshot keeps mean ± SE in each original cell. Values are fixed-tissue masses in grams, converted to mg only in the reader. The whole brain was dissected and all pieces of each structure summed. Amygdala plus pyriform cortex remains a combined region. No tissue-density assumption converts mass to volume.

Methods and sample sizes are shared with Table I. Tissue preparation differs between sheep/humans and the other taxa. The sum of dissected weights excludes choroid plexus, large vessels, ventricular fluid and meningeal remnants, unlike whole-brain weight. No additive total is printed in Table II, so no sum-to-total claim is tested. These records enter the source database and variable catalog as mass measures.
