# Miller 2017 Table 9

Source: Miller 2017 dissertation, **Table 9. Specimens used in this study**, printed p. 58 (PDF p. 63). The snapshot captures all 27 individually coded macaque, galago, and squirrel-monkey rows, including stain, side, degree of trauma, and recovery period. The table caption says percentages are available only for some lesions, qualitative labels come from investigator notes, and `N/A` marks controls.

Run `Rscript Miller__2017/Miller__2017_Table9.R`; it reads the frozen snapshot and writes the item CSV and `__Public/comparative-data/hdl1803-13821_Table9.tsv`. Numeric lesion percent is filled only when a percent is printed; qualitative grades remain text. Recovery days are numeric only when printed. These lesion and control animals are not assumed to be the V1 cases of Table 3 merely because they share a genus. AI checked the snapshot against PDF p. 63 on 2026-11-24; human signoff is pending. No figure data were extracted.

The open-access Qi et al. (2023) cuneate study (DOI 10.1073/pnas.2222076120) includes individual areal measurements in its supplement and Vanderbilt repository hdl:1803/18005. Its sample may overlap these archived Kaas cases, but case-level identity has not been verified. Compare printed codes and lesion histories before building a shared-specimen link or merging measurements.
