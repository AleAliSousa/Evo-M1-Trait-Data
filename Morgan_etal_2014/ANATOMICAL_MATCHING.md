# Anatomical matching — 12 November 2026

Source: `Morgan_etal_2014.pdf`, Methods, "Stereology", and Fig. 1.
Previously published Nissl-based contours transferred onto glial-stained sections delineate the amygdaloid complex and five subdivisions: lateral, basal, accessory basal, central, and remaining nuclei. The remaining group includes anterior cortical/amygdaloid areas, lateral olfactory tract nucleus, periamygdaloid cortex, medial/posterior cortical nuclei, amygdalohippocampal area and intercalated nuclei. The analysis uses the whole-complex summary from Data S1, not lateral nucleus alone.

Named amygdala tissue is compatible with Heiss's amygdaloid-complex label, but the latter uses sparse spheres with unspecified nuclear weights. Do not describe the two measurements as identical masks. BNST is not part of this anatomical match.
