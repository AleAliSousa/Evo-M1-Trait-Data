# Neves_etal_2014_Table1

Table 1 | Cellular composition of afrotherian brains. Source: `Neves-2014-Cellular scaling rul.pdf`, PDF page 4, DOI in `__ReadMe.xlsx`.

## Build

The printed table is transposed — species as columns, measures as rows under abbreviated codes — and the snapshot keeps that layout; the build transposes it into one row per species. The five species are *Elephantulus myurus* (n = 2), *Petrodromus tetradactylus* (n = 2), *Amblysomus hottentotus* (n = 2), *Procavia capensis* (n = 2) and *Dendrohyrax dorsalis* (n = 1). For each of seven structures — whole brain, cerebral cortex excluding the hippocampus, hippocampus, cerebral cortex including the hippocampus (CxT), cerebellum, rest of brain, olfactory bulb — the table prints mass (g), number of neurons and number of other (non-neuronal) cells (both in millions, converted to absolute counts), neuronal and other-cell densities (cells/mg) and the other/neuron ratio, as species means ± SD. Whole brain excludes the olfactory bulb. The table prints no total cell numbers and no % neurons, so the analysis CSV has none. Body mass is printed as a single species average with no SD, so its SD column is empty. All values are whole-structure estimates: only one hemisphere of each brain was available, so the printed numbers are twice the single-hemisphere counts. Numbers of other cells were derived by the authors by subtraction. The table's footnote (the code legend) reads: "Cellular composition of the five Afrotherian species. All masses in grams. Numbers of cells in millions. MBD, body mass; MBR, brain mass; MCX, mass of the cerebral cortex (excluding the hippocampus); MHP, mass of the hippocampus; MCXT, mass of the cerebral cortex including the hippocampus; MCB, mass of the cerebellum; MROB, mass of the rest of the brain; MOB, mass of the olfactory bulb; N, number of neurons; O, number of other (non-neuronal) cells; DN, density of neurons (neurons/mg); DO, density of other cells (other cells/mg); O/N, ratio between numbers of other cells and neurons."

From the paper folder, run:

```sh
Rscript Neves_etal_2014_Table1_snapshot.R
Rscript Neves_etal_2014_Table1.R
```

The snapshot script captures the table from the frozen PMC full text (`fulltext.html`) and refuses to overwrite a differing frozen snapshot. It was run once for this build. On subsequent runs, run only `Rscript Neves_etal_2014_Table1.R`. The R script reads only its item snapshot for measurements and writes `Neves_etal_2014_Table1.csv` and the DOI-coded public TSV. The item definitions are in `reference_tables/`.

## Interpretation and checks

- The snapshot was cross-checked cell-for-cell against the publisher PDF (text layer and rendered page): all 215 value cells, 43 row labels and the species headings agree. The only rendering difference is documented in the snapshot script: the printed row labels carry subscripts, which PMC's HTML flattens into plain codes ("MBD", "DNBR", "O/NBR").
- The table's additive identities hold at printed precision: CxT = CX + HP and whole brain = CxT + cerebellum + rest of brain for mass, neurons and other cells (loosest cell 0.12%, *Petrodromus* brain mass — rounding of the printed means). Derived quantities recomputed from the printed means (O/N, densities) agree with the printed values to a median of 0.41%; the loosest cells are the *Elephantulus myurus* hippocampus values (O/N off 14.9%, neuronal density off 5.7%), where the printed SDs are very large relative to the means, so per-individual averaging accounts for the gap.
- Herculano-Houzel et al. (2015) re-report these five species in their Tables 1–5, citing Neves et al. (2014) for all 23 species-structure rows. Their Table 1 "Cerebral cortex" is the cerebral cortex **including** the hippocampus: all 60 Table 1 comparisons match this build's CxT block, not CX alone. Across all five tables, 214 comparisons have values on both sides and 201 agree within Neves et al.'s printed rounding. The 13 exceptions are listed in the definitions file; the substantial ones are on the 2015 side: the *Amblysomus hottentotus* whole-brain other-cells total in their Table 5 (46,631,000) is a digit transposition of the correct 46,361,000 (their own structure tables sum to 46,360,263); their Table 1 other-cells SD for *Petrodromus* cerebral cortex total (1,104,000) duplicates their *Elephantulus* value against Neves et al.'s printed 5,217,000; and their Table 4 olfactory-bulb neuronal-density SDs are exactly √2 times Neves et al.'s printed SDs for both n = 2 species, an SD/SEM-type inconsistency for that one measure. Which values the merge carries is a wiring decision, not a build decision.
- **Printed anomaly kept verbatim in the snapshot**: the *Procavia capensis* olfactory-bulb neuron count is printed inside typographic double quotes, "20.909" (millions), in both the PDF and PMC. The digits are unambiguous and consistent with the printed density and mass, so the build parses the number, guarded on the exact printed string. The quotes are not explained in the paper. Herculano-Houzel et al. (2015), Table 4, give this species' olfactory bulb n = 1, consistent with the missing SDs in all its bulb rows here.
- Olfactory bulb availability: *Dendrohyrax dorsalis* has no bulb rows; *Amblysomus hottentotus* has a bulb mass (0.050 g, no SD) but no cell counts (Herculano-Houzel et al. (2015) omit the species from their Table 4 entirely); the other three species have complete bulb rows.
- The paper's Results prose mis-states three of its own Table 1 density ranges: the hippocampus neuronal density maximum is given as 54,601 neurons/mg (a value that appears nowhere in the table; the table maximum is 77,004 in *E. myurus*), the cerebellum minimum as 175,610 neurons/mg in the rock hyrax (table: 188,180 in *D. dorsalis*; rock hyrax 242,415), and the cerebellum maximum of 531,494 neurons/mg is attributed to the golden mole (table: *E. myurus*; the golden mole value is 409,687). The cortex and rest-of-brain ranges and the other-cell extremes in the same paragraph are correct. The build follows the table throughout.
- The snapshot was checked by the assistant against the PDF text and layout; human visual signoff remains pending in the registry. These are source measurements, not a normal-versus-condition contrast. No merge is performed by this build.
