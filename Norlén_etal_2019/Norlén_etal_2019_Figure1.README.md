# Figure 1 — chimpanzee sugar concentration response

Norlén et al. 2019, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 32, Figure 1. The five panels show sucrose, fructose, glucose, maltose and lactose preference against water for three Western chimpanzees. Each plotted point is the mean of ten one-minute trials per animal. The paper does not give individual IDs; circle, square and triangle are retained as plotted series labels, without assigning animal identities. The x axis is logarithmic concentration in mM; the y axis is preference percent. Dotted reference levels are 50% chance and 66.7% preference criterion.

`Norlén_etal_2019_Figure1_snapshot.csv` records approximate point centres in a 1,489-pixel-wide rendering of the held source PDF, along with each panel's plotted axis endpoints. `Norlén_etal_2019_Figure1.R` reads that snapshot directly, converts the logarithmic x and linear y coordinates, and writes the item CSV and DOI-coded public TSV. The full concentration curves are distinct from the built Table 1 thresholds and Figures 2–3 categorical comparisons. Do not treat this as additional animals in a merge.

Ran `Rscript --vanilla Norlén_etal_2019/Norlén_etal_2019_Figure1.R` and `Rscript --vanilla Norlén_etal_2019/Norlén_etal_2019_Figure1_replot.R` on 2026-11-26. The separate replot reads the item CSV and preserves panel order, the three printed symbols, log x scale, percent y scale, and criterion lines. The generated PNG was inspected side by side with the source: the main curves, peaks, and thresholds follow the printed shape; some close/overlapping points remain approximate. The caption says mean ± SD, but individual SD whiskers are not discernible for reliable extraction in this rendering; no SD type or magnitude is invented. Replot inspection is an internal shape check, not human visual signoff.
