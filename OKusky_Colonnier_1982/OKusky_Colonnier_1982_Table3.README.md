# OKusky_Colonnier_1982_Table3

O'Kusky & Colonnier (1982), DOI 10.1002/cne.902100307. PDF p. 6, printed p. 283, Table 3. Mean laminar thickness and SD in µm from six hemispheres of two *M. fascicularis* and one *M. mulatta*. Species were pooled by the authors; `Species` is intentionally blank because these are not a species-specific estimate. Printed total is retained and its agreement with the laminar sum checked (rounding tolerance 1 µm).
