# OKusky_Colonnier_1982_Table4

O'Kusky & Colonnier (1982), DOI 10.1002/cne.902100307. PDF p. 6, printed p. 283, Table 4. Thirteen laminar rows plus the printed total. Source columns: A neurons/mm³ in thousands; B neurons under 1 mm² in thousands; C neurons under a 25 × 25 µm patch; D neurons in one hemisphere in millions. All ± values are **SD**, as printed in the footnote. The reader converts A, B, D to absolute counts and keeps the total row (not an independent animal).

The Figure 7 neuron point is the pooled mixed-species **160.85 ± 18.48 million** neurons in one area 17 hemisphere. It is not a value for either species alone. Pairing Table 2 mean surface and Table 3 mean thickness gives an approximate volume of 840.70 mm² × 1.5923 mm = 1,338.6 mm³; the cohorts for those tables differ, so this is not a directly measured volume for the Table 4 neurons. Do not label it as specimen-level volume. Charvet et al. (2013) regard this older count as lacking modern stereological correction; keep its method separate from optical-fractionator estimates.
