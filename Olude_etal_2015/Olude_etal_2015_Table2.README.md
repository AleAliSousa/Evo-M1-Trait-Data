# Olude et al. 2015 Table 2

Source: `Olude-2015-Astrocyte morphology, heterogeneity.pdf`, Table 2 on PDF
page 7. The snapshot preserves all 17 printed brain-region rows in order,
juvenile and adult cells with their ± signs, and the seven significant-row
asterisks. The paper describes the values as mean ± SEM; each age group had
five male African giant rats (*Cricetomys gambianus*). The source describes
right-hemisphere sagittal-section astrocyte counts; these are not cells/mm³.
Age was estimated from body weight and sexual maturity because exact ages of
wild-caught juveniles/adults were not known. The abstract mentions 12 regions,
whereas the printed Table 2 has 17 rows; all 17 are retained.

Run `Rscript Olude_etal_2015/Olude_etal_2015_Table2.R` from the repo root to
rebuild the 17-row analysis CSV. The source page and snapshot were compared on
2026-11-26, and the reader checks all numeric cells and seven significance
marks. No human visual signoff is claimed. Owner review of the proposed
`Olude_etal_2015_Table2_registry_row.xlsx` is needed before the public TSV
can be generated.
