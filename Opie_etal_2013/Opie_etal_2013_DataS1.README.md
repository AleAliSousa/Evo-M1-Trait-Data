# Opie_etal_2013_DataS1

Opie, C., Atkinson, Q. D., Dunbar, R. I. M., & Shultz, S. (2013). Male infanticide leads to social monogamy in primates. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 110, 13328–13332. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1307903110

Untouched Supplementary Data Table workbook, Sheet1: rows5–234 are 230 primate tree-label records. Rows1–4 contain captions, units and column-wide source lists; rows238 onward supply the bibliography. The reader fills down the printed Family grouping but retains species tree labels and all categorical codes. DD means data deficient; the workbook notes an infanticide evidence threshold of fewer than 20 publications. Published exceptions are retained, not recoded by applying a new threshold. “Total Publications” is literature coverage, not animals studied.

Column-level references and row-level additional references remain separate and join to the original workbook bibliography. They are not falsely assigned as exact per-cell observations. Gestation/weaning remain days; the supplied ratio L/(G+L) is checked with numerical precision tolerance. High/Low infanticide, Monogamous and other categories are author coding, not quantitative rates or claims about all populations. Missing, DD, Absent, No and Low remain distinct. These compiled behavioral/life-history covariates can support the project, with original operational definitions and source overlap retained.

The supplied digital workbook is preserved byte-for-byte. No PDF transcription check applies.

Rebuild with `Rscript Opie_etal_2013_DataS1.R` from any directory. The frozen source is read locally and the registry supplies the encoded public TSV name. Published taxon labels remain unchanged apart from explicitly described formatting. No global species key or unsupported hemisphere conversion is introduced. Source records enter the database and variable catalog; aggregate merge inputs are unchanged pending source/specimen and anatomical mapping.
