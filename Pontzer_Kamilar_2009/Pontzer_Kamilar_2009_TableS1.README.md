## Interpretation and provenance

161 species rows across ten order categories (eleven printed labels, counting the lowercase “primates” separately); missing traits are retained. Original body masses in kg are converted to g; distances stay km/day. Daily movement is not home-range area or direct energy expenditure. Litter mass is not individual offspring mass. Lifetime fertility/output are author-derived from reproductive lifespan and annual production, not primary measured energy use. Methods identify Carbone et al. (2005; ref6) and Ernest (2003; ref38) as compilation sources, but the table has no per-row original-source attribution. Existing primate day-range datasets may overlap. The wrapped Daubentonia madagascariensis label is rejoined in its original cell. Halichoerus grypus is printed under Artiodactyla; preserve and flag that classification, not silently correct it.
