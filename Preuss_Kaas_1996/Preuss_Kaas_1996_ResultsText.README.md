# Preuss & Kaas 1996 — Results text

Source: Preuss, T. M., & Kaas, J. H. (1996). Cytochrome oxidase “blobs” and other characteristics of primary visual cortex in a lemuroid primate, *Cheirogaleus medius*. *Brain, Behavior and Evolution, 47*(2), 103–112. https://doi.org/10.1159/000113231

The constructed snapshot preserves the reported V1 area (66 mm²; approximately 12% of the left cortical mantle) and the three median blob dimensions reported in the same passage. The specimen is one adult female, approximately 20 years old, whose brain was immersion-fixed after natural death; the paper notes cataracts and functional blindness. These values should not be treated as a species mean. Public export remains pending registry-row paste.
