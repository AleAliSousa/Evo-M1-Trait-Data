## Measurement and methods
**Serotonin-transporter-immunoreactive axon length density; not serotonin concentration, cell number, or synapse count.** Original units **µm/µm³** are retained in `ALv_unit`: length per tissue volume, dimension L⁻², not cell number density and not tissue volume in mm³. No unit conversion, total axon length inference, neuron-count conversion, or ALv/Nv normalization is made, including layer I.

Macaques: transcardial perfusion with 4% paraformaldehyde. Humans/chimpanzees: postmortem immersion in 10% buffered formalin for 7–10 days. Fixation differences are preserved, not silently corrected. This paper reports 0.1% sodium azide storage (do not replace it with another marker paper's method). Sections were cut at 40 µm. Antibody: Rabbit anti-SERT polyclonal antibody directed at C-terminus of rat SERT peptide 596–622; gift of Randy D. Blakely; primary incubation 1:12 000; 48 hours at 4°C. Antigen retrieval: 10 mM sodium citrate buffer, pH 8.5; 90°C; 30 minutes; preblocking 4% normal goat serum; 0.3% Triton X-100; 3% dried milk. The immunostained sampling series differs by species/block; see context. Length was estimated stereologically with SpaceBalls (10 µm sampling hemispheres; StereoInvestigator v.6) and divided by the sampled reference volume. This sampled region does not establish whole-area total innervation.
