## Measurement and methods
**Tyrosine hydroxylase-immunoreactive axon length density. TH is not 100% dopamine-specific; it also participates in norepinephrine and epinephrine synthesis. The study did not perform TH-antibody colocalization analyses (pp. 6–7). Never label these values total dopamine or dopaminergic cell counts.** Original units **µm/µm³** are retained in `ALv_unit`: length per tissue volume, dimension L⁻², not cell number density and not tissue volume in mm³. No unit conversion, total axon length inference, neuron-count conversion, or ALv/Nv normalization is made, including layer I.

Macaques: transcardial perfusion with 4% paraformaldehyde. Humans/chimpanzees: postmortem immersion in 10% buffered formalin for 7–10 days. Fixation differences are preserved, not silently corrected. This paper reports 0.01% sodium azide storage (do not replace it with another marker paper's method). Sections were cut at 40 µm. Antibody: Rabbit anti-TH polyclonal antibody; AB152, Chemicon; putative DA-containing fibers; primary incubation 1:1,000; 48 hours at 4°C. Antigen retrieval: 10 mM sodium citrate buffer, pH 3.5; 37°C; 30 minutes; preblocking 2% normal goat serum; 0.3% Triton X-100. The immunostained sampling series differs by species/block; see context. Length was estimated stereologically with SpaceBalls (10 µm sampling hemispheres; StereoInvestigator v.6) and divided by the sampled reference volume. This sampled region does not establish whole-area total innervation.
