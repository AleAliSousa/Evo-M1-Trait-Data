# Reep & O'Shea 1990 — Table 3 (cortical data from spaced sections in 2 Florida manatee brains)

Reep RL, O'Shea TJ (1990). Brain Behav Evol 35(4):185-194. doi:10.1159/000115866. PDF p.6 (journal p.190); scanned, no text layer; transcribed snapshot `..._Table3_snapshot.csv` (2 brains + printed Mean; columns: GI, cortical surface area left/right/total, cm2).

## Fidelity checks
- left + right = total for both brains (56+60=116; 68+69=137); the Mean row differs by 1 (62+64 vs 127; unrounded means 62, 64.5, 126.5).
- Mean row = mean of the two brains: GI 1.06, left 62, total 127 (right 64.5 printed 64).

## Caveats
M-387 (coronal sections) and 84-14 (horizontal sections) are different brains and plane; GI is cortical perimeter / exposed perimeter. Species not printed (caption "Florida manatees"); `species` = *Trichechus manatus* from the text. Lewitus 2014 (ref 83): GI / cortical surface area comparables.
