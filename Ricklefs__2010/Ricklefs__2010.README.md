# Ricklefs 2010 — Life-history connections to rates of aging in terrestrial vertebrates (NOT YET BUILT)

Ricklefs, R. E. (2010). Life-history connections to rates of aging in terrestrial vertebrates. *Proceedings of the
National Academy of Sciences*, 107(22), 10314–10319. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1005862107 (PMC2890449)

## Why this item exists
"Ricklefs, 2010" (the Amniote database's `Ricklefs, 2010b`) is the fourth-largest feeder of `Myhrvold_etal_2015`:
3,287 records over 620 species (2,066 of them mammal cells), for adult mass, longevity, female maturity, gestation,
weaning and birth mass. The species values are in the paper's supplementary information, not in the main text.

## Status: blocked on the source file
- Neither the article PDF nor its supplement is in the owner's EndNote library.
- The supplement is listed on PMC as `1005862107_sapp.pdf` (PNAS "SI Appendix"). Both PMC and pnas.org serve a
  browser challenge to scripted downloads, so it could not be fetched automatically (2026-10-23).
- **To unblock:** download, in a browser, the article PDF and the SI Appendix from
  https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1005862107 (Supplementary Materials) or
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2890449/. Save them in this folder unchanged. If the SI ships a
  spreadsheet ("Dataset S1"), that file is the frozen source (copy-renamed `..._snapshot.<ext>`). If it is only the
  PDF, the tables are extracted to a `_snapshot.csv` as for `Savage_etal_2004`.
- The registry row uses `Item number = SI Appendix` **provisionally**. Reset col D to the printed table label once
  the SI has been seen, before the first build.
