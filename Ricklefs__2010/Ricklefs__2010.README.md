# Ricklefs 2010 — folder index

Ricklefs, R. E. (2010). Life-history connections to rates of aging in terrestrial vertebrates. *Proceedings of the
National Academy of Sciences*, 107(22), 10314–10319. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1005862107 (PMC2890449)

Two items are built from the Supporting Information (`sapp.pdf`):
- `Ricklefs__2010_Appendix4.README.md`: SI Appendix 4, Weibull mortality parameters and the rate of actuarial aging
  ω (Ricklefs's own fits to ISIS zoo data; role: both).
- `Ricklefs__2010_Appendix6.README.md`: SI Appendix 6, life-history variables copied from AnAge (role: secondary).

The other SI appendices (1–3, 5, 7–10) are regression summaries, model notes and contrasts, not species tables.
The related `Ricklefs, R.E., 2010a` (Embryo growth rates in birds and mammals, *Functional Ecology* 24:588–596;
36 Amniote records) is a different paper and is not built.
