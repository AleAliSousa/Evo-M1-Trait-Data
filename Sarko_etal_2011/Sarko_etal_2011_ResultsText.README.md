# Sarko et al. 2011 — Results text

Source: Sarko, D. K., Leitch, D. B., Girard, I., Sikes, R. S., & Catania, K. C. (2011). Organization of somatosensory cortex in the northern grasshopper mouse (*Onychomys leucogaster*), a predatory rodent. *Journal of Comparative Neurology, 519*(1), 64–74. https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.22504

`Sarko_etal_2011_ResultsText_snapshot.csv` is a constructed frozen snapshot of the six cortical-area summaries reported on p. 68: total neocortex, V1, and the posteromedial barrel subfield (PMBSF) of S1 for juveniles and adults. Values are mean ± SD; the source's approximate percentages are retained only where printed. Public export remains pending registry-row paste.
