# Semendeferi 1994 Chapter 3 quantified specimens

Source: Katerina Semendeferi, *Evolution of the Hominoid Prefrontal Cortex: A Quantitative and Image Analysis of Areas 13 and 10* (University of Iowa dissertation, December 1994; UMI 9525195), Chapter 3 Materials and Methods, printed pp. 53–56 (PDF pp. 77–80). No single specimen table is printed. The constructed snapshot tabulates the ten explicitly described quantified specimens in the order of the prose, preserving codes, animal names, sex, age text, body weight, brain weight and whether the weight is stated fresh or fixed. The subject material is mixed Van Hoesen, AFIP and Yerkes/Zilles collection material; source-specific collection detail is in printed p. 52.

Run `Rscript Semendeferi__1994/Semendeferi__1994_Chapter3SpecimensText.R`; it reads only the frozen snapshot and writes the item CSV and `__Public/comparative-data/UMI9525195_Chapter3SpecimensText.tsv`. kg are converted to g, the one `110 lbs` body weight to rounded g, and 16 years 5 months to decimal years. Brain weights without an explicit fresh/fixed qualifier are marked `unspecified`; SN 31/84's fixed **left hemisphere** weight is separate from whole-brain weight. Unknowns remain missing.

The dissertation explicitly names `YN86-137` as **Mulika**, `YN82-140` as **Jini**, and `YN85-38` as **Kanting**, and prints the last as *Pongo pygmaeus abelii*. These are public identity links to existing specimen records, including the orangutan currently also documented by restricted correspondence. A separate inconsistency is preserved: the summary on printed p. 53 says gibbon `YN81/136`, while its detailed account on p. 56 says `YN81-146`. The latter is the snapshot's row; the former remains unresolved, and no identity with `YN81-136` is asserted. AI checked the snapshot against PDF pp. 77–80 on 2026-11-24; human signoff is pending. No figure data were extracted.

The dissertation's later brain/area 13 and brain/area 10 volumes (printed Table 4, p. 182) recur in the already built `Semendeferi_etal_1998_TABLE2` and `Semendeferi_etal_2001_TABLE2`. The dissertation's new value here is the individual specimen identity and metadata, not an independent volume replicate. The published tables should remain the measurement sources. The dissertation's separate `Chimp 1` is not silently assigned to the quantified area 13/10 chimpanzee row; the summary names chimpanzee `1548` for that series.
