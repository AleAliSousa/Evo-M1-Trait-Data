# Sheridan et al. 2012 Figure 1

Source: `Sheridan-2012-Variation in neural development.pdf`, Figure 1 on PDF
page 2. The caption describes group means in cubic centimeters and error bars
of ±1 SEM. The snapshot records approximate bar-top and error-cap pixels from
the embedded image, in CAU, FCG, NIG order, with its 400 and 600 cc axis
calibration. Run `Rscript Sheridan_etal_2012/Sheridan_etal_2012_Figure1.R`
to regenerate the three-row analysis CSV, then
`Rscript Sheridan_etal_2012/Sheridan_etal_2012_Figure1_replot.R` to save
`Sheridan_etal_2012_Figure1_replot.png` beside it. The replot reads the CSV
directly and preserves bar order, approximate heights, SEM caps, and axis scale.

The source image and replot were inspected on 2026-11-26. The bars reproduce
the figure's approximate shape; significance brackets and printed adjusted
regression annotations are omitted from the replot and remain in the source
figure/Table 1. Pixel placement is approximate, so these are not author-tabulated
means. No human visual signoff is claimed. Clinical arms must remain distinct.

registry-row workbook of the same item name.
