# Anatomical matching beyond thalamus — 12 November 2026

Source: `Sjolin-2022-Distribution of five clinically im.pdf`, Methods and regional Table 2; supplementary Methods in `13041_2022_935_MOESM1_ESM.docx`.
The material consists of small tissue samples, not complete-structure homogenates. Cortex and lobar white matter are distinguished, but mesencephalon and cerebellum labels do not provide a quantified nuclear or grey/white composition. Do not describe them as known whole-midbrain/whole-cerebellum mixtures.

The primary analysis retains four cortical lobes, caudate, hippocampus and internal capsule as sampled named-structure/tissue matches. It excludes unspecified thalamic tissue, mesencephalon and cerebellum from Heiss matching. Pooling lobar white matter does not make it centrum semiovale. See `THALAMIC_ANATOMY.md` for the remaining thalamic uncertainty.
