# Soto et al. 2022 source disposition

Soto, C., Gázquez, J. M. M., & Llorente, M. (2022). Hand preferences in coordinated bimanual tasks in non-human primates: A systematic review and meta-analysis. *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews*, 141, 104822. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2022.104822).

The exact article PDF and publisher supplementary DOCX are held here. Supplementary Tables S3–S6 were built on 2026-11-24 as **secondary species moderator estimates**: S3 and S4 report signed handedness index for tube and other bimanual tasks; S5 and S6 report absolute handedness index for those task categories. Each item has its own frozen XLSX, R reader, analysis CSV, public TSV, definitions and README. The published number of studies is not an animal sample size. Out-of-scale source values and confidence bounds are retained and flagged. These estimates may overlap Caspar et al. 2022 and Püschel et al. 2026 study samples, so they are not independent primary observations and are not merged as such.

Article PDF Tables 1–2 remain optional study/species/sample-size locators. They do not print individual HI/MHI observations. The study-level source data or primary papers are still needed for direct provenance checks of Püschel’s non-Caspar rows.
