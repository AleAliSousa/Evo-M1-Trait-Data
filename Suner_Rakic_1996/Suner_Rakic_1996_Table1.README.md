# Suner_Rakic_1996_Table1

Suner & Rakic (1996), *Visual Neuroscience* 13:585–590, DOI 10.1017/S0952523800008269. PDF p. 2, printed p. 586, Table 1. Five male macaques, both hemispheres, in source age order. The frozen snapshot retains the printed LGN and area 17 columns and their **95% confidence interval half-widths**, in millions. The reader converts counts to absolute neurons.

Figure 7 of Charvet et al. (2013) uses the area 17 counts but places them at Giannaris & Rosene's mean V1 volume; Suner & Rakic did not publish the corresponding individual V1 volumes in Table 1. Do not infer Suner volumes from the figure. Run: `Rscript --vanilla Suner_Rakic_1996/Suner_Rakic_1996_Table1.R`. AI visually checked the table against the PDF on 2026-11-24; human signoff pending. No figure-derived dataset or replot.

On 2026-11-26 the build was pointed at its existing row in the main `__ReadMe.xlsx` registry (rather than the staging workbook) and rerun. It wrote 10 analysis rows and the public file `__Public/comparative-data/10.1017%2Fs0952523800008269_Table1.tsv`. The printed 95% confidence-interval half-widths remain labeled as such; they are not SD or SEM.
