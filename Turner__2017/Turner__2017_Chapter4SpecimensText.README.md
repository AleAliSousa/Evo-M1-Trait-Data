# Turner 2017 Chapter 4 identified pinniped cases

Source: Turner 2017 dissertation, Chapter 4 Materials and Methods, printed pp. 82–83 (PDF pp. 97–98). The study used two male northern elephant seal pups, approximately two months old, and three California sea lion pups, two female and one male, approximately 9–10 months old. The methods print two case codes for dissected cortices: elephant seal `15-16` and sea lion `16-14`. The other three animals are unnamed; the group sex and age descriptions **cannot** be assigned to either identified case individually.

The constructed snapshot contains only the two printed case IDs with their species and tissue use. Run `Rscript Turner__2017/Turner__2017_Chapter4SpecimensText.R`; it reads that snapshot and writes the item CSV and `__Public/comparative-data/hdl1803-11720_Chapter4SpecimensText.tsv`. The analysis retains the group context as text and does not invent individual sex or exact age. AI checked the snapshot against PDF pp. 97–98 on 2026-11-24; human signoff is pending. No figure data were extracted.

Published follow-up: Turner, Sawyer & Kaas (2017), DOI 10.1002/cne.24188, reports the elephant seal and sea lion visual systems. The paper is not built here; its case identities and any numeric tables should be checked against this dissertation chapter before ingestion.
