# Turner 2017 Table 3.1

Source: Turner 2017 dissertation, Chapter 3, **Table 3.1 Summary of experimental cases**, printed p. 50 (PDF p. 65). The snapshot preserves the six printed hemisphere rows and all seven source columns. `12-58 LH` and `12-58 RH` are two hemispheres of the **same** male *Macaca radiata*, so the table represents five animals. The PHA footnote means *Papio hamadryas anubis*; PHX is an anubis/cynocephalus hybrid. No single-species assignment is imposed on PHX.

The R reader removes only the hemisphere suffix to create `case_id`, retains each row, and converts brain surface area from cm² to mm² (×100). Its `brain_weight_g_as_published` column keeps the source's wording and hemisphere-row context. Run `Rscript Turner__2017/Turner__2017_Table3.1.R`; outputs are the item CSV and `__Public/comparative-data/hdl1803-11720_Table3.1.tsv`. The definitions and registry row proposal are in this folder. Young et al. 2013's baboons `09-27` and `11-31` are already in the crosswalk; the exact case codes support cross-paper matching. AI checked the transcription against PDF p. 65 on 2026-11-24; human signoff is pending. No figure data were extracted.

**Published duplicate:** all six case/hemisphere rows reproduce the age, sex, brain weight, and cortical surface area in `Turner_etal_2016_Table1` (allowing the dissertation's `09-27` versus paper's `9-27` typography).
