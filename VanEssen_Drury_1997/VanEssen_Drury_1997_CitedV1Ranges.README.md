# Van Essen & Drury 1997 — cited human V1 ranges in Results text

This item captures the two **secondary** ranges printed on page 11 of the Van Essen & Drury PDF, Results section “Estimating the extent of area V1”: Stensaas et al. 1974 report 15–37 cm², and Filiminoff 1932 report 22–29 cm² for V1 in one human hemisphere. The printed cm² endpoints and their explicit mm² conversions are recorded with the cited primary paper named in each row.

The Kaskan workbook's derived midpoints are excluded. This item is reference provenance only and must not enter a pooled V1 surface merge alongside primary data. It is not a verification of the cited primary papers.

The source paper PDF is already in this folder. A scan of Filiminoff (spelled Filimonoff in the EndNote filename) 1932 was found in the local EndNote library and copied to `Filiminoff_1932/` for a future primary-source extraction. No Stensaas 1974 PDF was found in the local EndNote PDF directory; its DOI is [10.3171/jns.1974.40.6.0747](https://doi.org/10.3171/jns.1974.40.6.0747). Neither cited primary has been represented here as an independently verified case-level dataset.
