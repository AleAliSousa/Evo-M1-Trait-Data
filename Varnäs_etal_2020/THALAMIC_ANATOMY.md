# Thalamic anatomy — Varnäs et al. 2020

The Table 1 **Thalamus** value is an autoradiographic regional ROI measurement. It is not established as a volume-weighted whole-thalamus estimate, nor as a mediodorsal-only measurement.

## Primary evidence

- `Varnas-2020-Autoradiographic mapping of synapt.pdf`, PDF pp. 2–3, sections 2.2.1–2.2.2: human whole hemispheres were cryosectioned into 100-µm coronal or horizontal sections; binding was quantified in regional ROIs. This specifies tissue preparation, not exhaustive sampling of every thalamic nucleus.
- PDF p. 4, Table 1: the human Thalamus row has n=3 and regional specific binding 541 (range 482–599). A nucleus-specific numeric breakdown is not printed there.
- PDF p. 4, Fig. 2: human autoradiograms identify anterior, mediodorsal, reticular, ventral-anterior and ventral-lateral thalamic territories. Fig. 1 is non-human primate and additionally labels the lateral geniculate and pulvinar; avoid transferring a label between species panels.
- PDF p. 4, Results: thalamic binding is heterogeneous, with higher binding in anterior and mediodorsal regions and lower binding in reticular and lateral-geniculate regions across the displayed material.

## Consequence for matching

The precise weighting and boundaries underlying the Table 1 Thalamus average are not given. Keep it as a broad thalamic ROI with unresolved subregional composition. The images show that nucleus-specific analysis may be possible, but no human MDT numeric value should be invented from qualitative staining or assigned the table's aggregate value. The existing Table1 README already mentions the figure-only nuclear information; this note specifies the matching limits.
