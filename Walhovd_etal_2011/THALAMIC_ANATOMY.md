# Thalamic anatomy — Walhovd et al. 2011

The reported thalamus volumes are **aggregate FreeSurfer thalamus segmentations**, not mediodorsal or geniculate volumes.

## Primary evidence

- `Walhovd_etal_2011.pdf`, printed p. 921 / PDF p. 6, section 2.3 Volumetric analyses: FreeSurfer 4.0.1 assigns a neuroanatomical label to each MRI voxel using a manually labelled training set; an atlas-based normalization procedure is described.
- The same page's Fig. 1 illustrates whole-brain segmentation.
- PDF pp. 8–9, Table 3: Thalamus is reported as one regional volume per demographic stratum, alongside other gross structures. No thalamic-nucleus parcellation is reported.

## Consequence for matching

The broad anatomical concept aligns with Mackes's aggregate thalamus volume, but the studies use different FreeSurfer versions (4.0.1 versus 6.0.0), populations and acquisition protocols. These should not be claimed to produce identical boundaries or remove cross-study calibration concerns. Neither source identifies the outcome as a specific medial thalamic nucleus. Keep the volume comparison distinct from the separate problem of finding a matched whole-thalamus metabolic rate.
