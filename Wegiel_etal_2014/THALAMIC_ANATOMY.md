# Thalamic anatomy — Wegiel et al. 2014

The reported stereological Thalamus is a **structure-level histological ROI in one hemisphere**, not a separately reported mediodorsal or geniculate nucleus.

## Primary evidence

- `Wegiel-2014-Stereological study of the neurona.pdf`, PDF p. 3, Delineation of regions of interest: anatomical boundaries are referred to an earlier report (reference 35); this article does not repeat a thalamus-specific boundary prescription.
- PDF pp. 4–5, Figs. 1–2: Th labels a gross thalamic outline in histological sections.
- PDF p. 6, stereology Methods and Table 1: six sampled sections per case for thalamus, with an optical-fractionator counting design. This is sampling for an estimated ROI total, not six independently measured nuclei.
- Tables 2–3 report one Thalamus volume and neuronal count/density row. The compiled data distinguish absolute processed-tissue volume from shrinkage-corrected volume.

## Interpretation and limits

It is reasonable to treat this as a gross thalamic structure estimate, but the current article alone does not establish exactly which peripheral territories (including geniculate or reticular components) are inside the delineation. The earlier delineation reference needs to be resolved before claiming exact identity with MRI whole-thalamus masks. Hemisphere side is not specified in the item.

Do not substitute the table's total or density for a mediodorsal-only measurement. Pair counts and processed reference volume consistently when reconstructing density; do not divide them by a different source's whole-thalamus or shrinkage-corrected volume without an explicit change of estimand.
