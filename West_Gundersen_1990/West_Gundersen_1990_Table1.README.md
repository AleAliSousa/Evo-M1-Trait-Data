# West_Gundersen_1990_Table1

West, M. J., & Gundersen, H. J. G. (1990). Unbiased stereological estimation of the number of neurons in the human hippocampus. Journal of Comparative Neurology, 296, 1–22. https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.902960102

Table 1 (p.3): the five male donors used in Table 2. Published patient numbers and unique ages allow the tables to be joined. Brain grams become mg and body kg becomes g. The table includes each cause of death; Materials state no signs of neurological disorder before death. These are five donors, not a separate sample from the neuronal data. Brains were fixed in 4% formalin for 6–12 weeks after autopsy. No arithmetic totals are printed.
