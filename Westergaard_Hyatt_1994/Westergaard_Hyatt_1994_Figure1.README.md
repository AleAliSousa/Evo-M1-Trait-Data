# Westergaard & Hyatt 1994 — Figure 1

Westergaard and Hyatt (1994), *Human Evolution* 9:273–279, doi:10.1007/BF02435514. The local source PDF is `Westergaard-1994-The responses of bonobos (Pan.pdf`; Figure 1 is on printed p.274/PDF p.2. The study tested nine bonobos in mirror-on and mirror-off conditions. Figure 1 plots the **difference in adjusted frequency scores per subject**, not an individual-animal count or a mark-test pass rate.

The six bar tops were read from a 170-dpi source-page render. `Westergaard_Hyatt_1994_Figure1_snapshot.csv` records source order, behavior labels, pixel positions, axis anchors, and printed significance stars. `Rscript --vanilla Westergaard_Hyatt_1994/Westergaard_Hyatt_1994_Figure1.R` writes the six-row local CSV and DOI-coded public TSV; values are approximate to one decimal. The Results text supplies mirror-on and mirror-off means for Mouth, Face, Contingent Action, and Inactive Looking. Their differences (2.6, 2.2, 2.2, 13.7) agree with the approximate bars (2.5, 2.2, 2.2, 13.7) within 0.1 score unit. No error bars are printed. The other two bars have no exact text values.
