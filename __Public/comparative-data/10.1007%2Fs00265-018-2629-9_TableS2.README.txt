Table S2 List of plant species and plant parts foraged and fed on by E. rubriventer between September 2012 and May 2013 at three sites in RNP. Table includes the percentage of foraging bouts accounted for by each plant taxon overall (% All) and for each site (TK = Talatakely, VL = Valohoaka, VT = Vatoharanana). Plant parts/species included in color modeling analyses are highlighted in grey. Ripeness of fruit is identified in bold if it was consumed in >50% of fruit foraging bouts when both unripe and ripe were consumed. Plant parts identified with “?” were present during a bout, but had not been directly observed to be consumed. (FR = Fruit, RFR = Ripe Fruit, MR = Mid-Ripe Fruit, UFR = Unripe Fruit, BD = Flower Bud, FL = Flower, YL = Young Leaf, ML = Mature Leaf)

aDendemy flowers were observed to be tasted (placed into the mouth) but not consumed, although this may represent nectar consumption.
bDuring behavioral data collection, ripeness of fruit consumed could not be accurately determined for this species. However, when collected ripe fruit could be distinguished from unripe fruit based on differences in size and color. 
cDuring the study, the terms “Fatsikahitra” and “Kalafana” were used to describe multiple plant taxa. Once this was noted, distinctions were made. Therefore, prior to the distinctions, these terms may include multiple taxa. However, “Fatsikahitra Unspecified” is likely overrepresented by Fatsikahitra sp. 1, Fatsikahitra sp. 7, and Fatsikahitra sp. 8. “Kalafana Unspecified” is likely overrepresented by Kalafana Small.
