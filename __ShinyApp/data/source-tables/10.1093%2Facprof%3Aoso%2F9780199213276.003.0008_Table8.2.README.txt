Table 8.2. Orangutan diet at eight sites (caption says eight; nine are printed). Both an overall figure and the mean are presented. As different studies use different methods for calculating means, we presented ranges in three 
different ways: (1) monthly ranges are the highest and lowest monthly values recorded; (2) trimester ranges are the highest and lowest values for 
3-month periods; (3) Values for low fruit are the mean value of all months classed as low fruit, ditto for high fruit. Monthly ranges were larger than 
trimester ranges in all cases. Three studies have combined flowers with other categories, marked as (inc. fl) in the table.
1 Fox et al. (2004); 2 Wich  et al. (2006a); 3 Galdikas (1988); 4 Rodman, (1988); 5 estimated from Fig. 19 in MacKinnon (1974).
