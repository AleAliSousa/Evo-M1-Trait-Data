# Dissertation measurement audit — 2026-11-25

**Source precedence:** peer-reviewed publications always take priority over dissertations for the same specimens and endpoint. Dissertations supply otherwise unpublished measurements and additional specimen identities, methods or provenance; they are never the preferred version of a result also published in a journal. Repeated specimen measurements are source relationships, not new animals. This audit supersedes the pending-action descriptions in `dissertation_specimen_followup_2026-11-24.csv`. It is a build queue; unbuilt items below are **not** in the public comparative TSVs.

## Interpretation notes

- For all linked cases, preserve the source's specimen label and link aliases in the crosswalk only with evidence. A shared genus or institution is insufficient.
- For Schenker Chapter III, distinguish raw/corrected volume and density. Table 3.2 neuronal counts are single measurements per case/hemisphere/area, even if multiple density versions are printed.
- Qi SI and the two public raw workbooks are now local and built. The 18 Miller roster overlaps are verified by exact case ID after separator normalization, but published Qi **area differences** do not supersede Miller's cuneate **volume** endpoint. Raw section sheets include repeated raters and revisions; do not treat them as independent animals or derive published ratios by naive pooling.
- No new figure-derived dataset was created in this audit; replot requirements apply when one is built.
