# de Magalhães & Costa 2009 — AnAge as published in 2009 (CITATION ONLY)

de Magalhães, J. P., & Costa, J. (2009). A database of vertebrate longevity records and their relation to other
life-history traits. *Journal of Evolutionary Biology*, 22(8), 1770–1774.
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1420-9101.2009.01783.x

## Why this item exists
"de Magalhaes and Costa, 2009" is the single largest feeder of the Amniote database (`Myhrvold_etal_2015`):
~23,400 records over 3,598 species (13,355 mammal cells). Myhrvold et al. cite the paper, but the values are the
AnAge build they downloaded, not anything printed in the paper.

## What the paper contains
The paper is a 5-page description of AnAge (4,122 entries at the time). It includes text-level comparisons with
Carey & Judge (2000) and Ernest (2003): 907 species are shared with Ernest, 643 of them have a higher longevity in
AnAge, and 48 differ more than two-fold in other traits, mostly adult mass. **It prints no species data table and does
not refer to a data supplement.** The data were "freely available online" as XML or tab-delimited downloads.

## Status: citation only (owner decision 2026-10-23)
Not built. The current AnAge (Build 15, 2023) is already in this repository as `deMagalhães_etal_2024`
(`deMagalhães_etal_2024_anagedata.txt`); use it for AnAge comparisons. HAGRID IDs are not stable across builds,
so join by binomial. A historical (2009-era) build is obtained only if it becomes necessary. Note that Ricklefs
(2010) SI Appendix 6 reprints AnAge life-history values for ~370 species as they stood c. 2009-2010
(`Ricklefs__2010/sapp.pdf`). The registry row keeps `Item number = AnAge build 2009` as a placeholder.
